Our story

Terracotta started in 2016 as a small neighbourhood trattoria. We believe in slow food, local farmers and the joy of sharing a table.

Every plate is a reflection of the seasons — from hand-rolled pasta to vegetables roasted in our wood-fired oven. Come as you are, stay a while.
